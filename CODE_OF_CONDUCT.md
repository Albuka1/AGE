# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior: empathy, respect, constructive feedback,
accepting responsibility, focusing on community.

Unacceptable behavior: sexualized language, trolling, harassment,
publishing private info, unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement

Report to Albuka1@users.noreply.github.com. All complaints reviewed promptly.
